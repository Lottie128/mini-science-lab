# Brag Plan: Mini Science Lab

## What is this app?
Mini Science Lab is an offline-first, child-friendly science learning app for kids aged 6–14 featuring 10 hands-on household experiments, 7 interactive canvas simulations, 6 STEM quizzes, and an animated robot mascot—running 100% without internet or servers.

## The angle
"Your kitchen is now an accredited particle physics lab."
A playful, wholesome, energetic launch video showing real working interactive physics (circuits, orbits, plant botany) and household experiments with zero ads, zero logins, and zero internet required.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on a vibrant sky-blue canvas: "Turn your kitchen table into a science laboratory."
The friendly robot mascot waves with sparkling glowing eyes while a beaker bubbles.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Live Interactive Circuit Lab (3-8s)**: An interactive breadboard appears. The switch snaps closed—bright cyan electrons surge through the wire—and the light bulb blooms into brilliant warm amber light with status: "Loop Complete • 3.0V Power!"
- **Beat 2: Real Household Experiments & Safety First (8-13s)**: Quick sequence: Magnet Magic checklist, safety shield badge ("Adult Supervision Verified"), and before/after color-changing turmeric solution test.
- **Beat 3: Solar Orbits & Plant Botany (13-17s)**: 8 planets revolving around a glowing Sun with speed controls, followed by a seed germinating, shooting roots, and blooming into fruit.

## Outro / punchline (17-20s)
"No internet connection? No problem."
The offline badge flashes green with 100% checkmarks, confetti explodes, "+50 XP Level Up!" crown appears, and the app title locks with:
"Mini Science Lab — Explore • Experiment • Learn • Grow!"

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Pick Scientist Avatar & launch Home Dashboard.
2. **Key Action**: Connect wires & close the switch in the Circuit Simulator to power the bulb.
3. **Result**: Trophy fanfare, XP points earned, and offline mastery unlocked!

## Tone
- **Preset**: `default` (playful, punchy, clean, postable)
- **Creative direction**: Wholesome kid inventor launch trailer — energetic, bright, science-powered.
- **Interpretation**: Pacing is quick and rhythmic, but text holds 1.5–2s settled for readability. Colors are vibrant cyan, sunshine yellow, and mint green with tactile audio chimes.

## Format & Specs
- **Format**: Landscape (`1920x1080`) / Responsive Web Player
- **Duration**: 20 seconds (1200 frames @ 60fps)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
- **Visual**: Glowing radial background `#0284c7` to `#38bdf8`. The Robot Mascot waves with smiling digital eyes.
- **Text**: "MINI SCIENCE LAB" → "Turn your kitchen table into a science lab."
- **Audio intent**: Cheerful intro riser and bubbly chime.

### Scene 2 — Hands-On Virtual Circuit (3.5s – 8.5s)
- **Visual**: Cut to the interactive circuit board. Battery (3.0V), copper wires, bulb, and knife switch.
- **Action**: Switch closes! Cyan electron dots zoom around the loop. The bulb lights up with radiant glow halo!
- **Text**: "Build working circuits • Watch electrons flow in real time"
- **Audio intent**: Switch snap + electrical hum + bright chime.

### Scene 3 — Household Science & Space (8.5s – 14.5s)
- **Visual**: Split showcase: 
  - Left: "Safe Household Experiments" with materials checklist and adult supervision badge.
  - Right: Solar system orbit viewer with planets revolving around the Sun.
- **Action**: Confetti burst + "+50 XP" reward star pill leaps onto screen.
- **Text**: "10 safe household experiments • 7 interactive labs"
- **Audio intent**: Rhythmic percussion groove + celebratory pop.

### Scene 4 — The Offline Guarantee & Outro (14.5s – 20.0s)
- **Visual**: WiFi icon with green shield: "100% Offline • Zero Accounts • Safe for Kids".
- **Action**: Mascot jumps in celebration with Level 5 golden crown.
- **Text**: "Mini Science Lab • Explore. Experiment. Learn."
- **Audio intent**: Full musical crescendo and victory fanfare!
